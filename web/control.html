Sponsors

The sponsors come from the GFX Engine's Global information (Global > Sponsors), one after another for their seconds, in CDL's place under the middle of the screen.

Players

The in-game names are read off the scoreboards while the CoD caster HUD is up. "Save IGN to player" links each one to their player in the GFX Engine's team roster (as in the VALORANT HUD), so the engine's graphics and the webcams know who they are.

Webcams

Each player runs the SCC Player Cam app on their PC, picks Call of Duty, connects to this PC and types their in-game name. Their cam shows, left of the player card, while the observer spectates them. A cam is matched by the name read off the scoreboard (or the roster player it's linked to); pick one by hand below if a name doesn't match.
